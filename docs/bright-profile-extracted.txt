1 8 · U G A N D A R E A L E S T A T E F A Q
Common questions from buyers.
Can foreigners legally own property in Uganda?
Yes — under the Condominium Property Act, foreign nationals can purchase and own residential
apartments with a separate certificate of title. Land is leasehold for non-citizens, commonly for 49 or 99
years.
 	What are typical rental yields in Kampala?
Luxury apartments in Nakasero, Kololo and Bugolobi typically yield 7%–11%, among the highest in East
Africa, with active dollar-denominated corporate rentals.
How do off-plan payment plans work?
Most developers offer interest-free plans during construction — a 10%–20% down payment, balance
spread over 12–24 months, and a final 10% on key handover.
 	What support does Bright Properties provide?
Full legal due diligence, structured payment-plan guidance, and property management alongside the
marketing itself.
